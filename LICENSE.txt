## 🔒 Privacy First Philosophy

This application was engineered with a **privacy-first architecture**. Because we firmly believe that your data belongs to you, all document manipulation is executed entirely **client-side** within your web browser. No document content, metadata, or user inputs are ever transmitted to an external server.
